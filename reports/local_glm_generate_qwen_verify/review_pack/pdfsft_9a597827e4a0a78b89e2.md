## Question

As part of a wind-tunnel data quality audit, use the Group 1 correlation in NASA TM 100019 to establish a reference lift-curve slope for the NACA 0012 at M = 0.3 and Re = 10^7. Report (a) the value of β·dCL/dα from the correlation, (b) the compressible lift-curve slope dCL/dα in per degree after applying the report's definition of β, (c) the correlation's validity limits and stated precision, and (d) whether a facility reporting β·dCL/dα = 0.0980 at these conditions would pass the report's Group 2 acceptance screening, showing the numerical margin.

## Gold answer

(a) From Eqn. 1, β·dCL/dα = 0.1025 + 0.00485·Log(10^7/10^6) = 0.1025 + 0.00485 = 0.10735 per degree. (b) With β = sqrt(1 - M²) = sqrt(1 - 0.09) = 0.9539 (Fig. 1 caption), dCL/dα = 0.10735/0.9539 ≈ 0.1125 per degree. (c) The correlation is a fit to Group 1 data over 2×10^6 < Re < 2×10^7 (subsonic, applied for M < 0.5 to within ±2%), with rms standard error 0.00024 and maximum error 0.0029 for the 30 points shown. (d) The facility value deviates by |0.10735 - 0.0980| = 0.00935, which exceeds the Group 2 tolerance of ±0.0040 in β·dCL/dα (and is about 8.7%, beyond the ±2% subsonic precision), so the facility data fail the acceptance screening.
