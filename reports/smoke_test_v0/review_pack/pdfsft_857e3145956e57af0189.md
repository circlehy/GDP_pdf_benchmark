### Governing data and method
- Zinc-air nominal cell voltage is **1.1 V**, so the minimum series count is `ceil(6.6/1.1) = 6 cells` [PDF p. 18, Table 3-1].
- Table 5-2 marks models **796, 1200, and 1204** with `**`; its footnote defines these as **“No longer available.”** They are therefore ineligible. The eligible models are **630** and **635** [PDF p. 45, Table 5-2 and footnote].
- Section 5.3.7.3 states that series connection raises voltage without raising nominal capacity, while paralleling raises capacity without raising nominal voltage [PDF p. 49]. Thus, for cell capacity `C`, the minimum parallel-string count is `ceil(6.0/C)`.
- Section 5.3.7.4 gives axial growth of **0.25–0.5 mm per cell** at complete discharge. Applying the required maximum gives **0.5 mm/cell**, or **3.0 mm** for each six-cell column [PDF p. 49].
- Table 5-2 weights are prior to discharge. Its footnote gives a theoretical oxygen-related increase of **0.2985 g O₂/A-h** [PDF p. 45]. Since every series cell undergoes its rated A-h discharge, added mass is `cell count × cell capacity × 0.2985`.
