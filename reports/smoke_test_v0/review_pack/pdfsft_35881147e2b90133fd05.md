### Audit of the Figure 15 band assertion

Figure 15 is for \(M=0.80\), \(\alpha=0\), with Reynolds number on the abscissa. Its dotted reference is labeled \(X_s=0.46\pm0.02\), hence the band is
\[
0.44\le X_s\le0.48.
\]
The legend identifies Lowe by a numbered square **□5** and Ohman by **□6**. [PDF p.14, Fig. 15]

The assertion that every eligible side-wall-suction source has all its points in the band is **false**. Lowe alone supplies a clear counterexample. Its most-forward (smallest-\(X_s\)) □5 point reads approximately
\[
X_s\approx0.41
\]
from the graph. Its departure from the nearest, lower band edge is therefore approximately
\[
0.44-0.41=0.03
\]
in plotted \(X_s\) units, i.e. about 0.03 forward of the band. This is a plot-derived estimate, not a tabulated value; reading the marker closer to 0.415 would give about 0.025, still clearly outside. The □6 Ohman markers appear near or within the band at the scan’s precision, but that cannot rescue the universal assertion once Lowe is outside.

### Benchmark disposition

**Do not accept these archives yet as ready-to-use CFD shock-position benchmarks.** Eligibility establishes only that Lowe and Ohman have relevant coverage, untripped testing, reported shock positions, and affirmative wall treatment. It does not prove a run at the exact combined condition, quantify the residual wall interference, or validate the underlying corrections. Moreover, the dotted interval is a descriptive reference band, not a demonstrated acceptance tolerance. The paper states that most values lie between 0.44 and 0.48 but calls the scatter disturbing and its cause a mystery; it specifically says that the experimental information needed for satisfactory CFD validation is lacking. [PDF p.13, “Shock-Wave Position”]

The summary likewise concludes that no single existing experiment is adequate for fully defining the NACA 0012 characteristics or validating CFD and that prescribed flow-quality and data-accuracy requirements were seldom met. [PDF p.14, “Summary and Conclusions”] Lowe and Ohman should therefore remain **candidate archives requiring run-level data, uncertainty documentation, and correction verification**; Takashima/Sawada remains excluded under the stated screen.
